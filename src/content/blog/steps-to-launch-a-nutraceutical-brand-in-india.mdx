Launching a supplement brand looks simple from the outside: find a factory, put your name on a pack, start selling. In practice there are seven steps, and skipping any of them is where most new brands lose time and money.

## 1. Start with your audience, not the product

The brands that sell fastest are built for people who already trust the founder: a fitness coach's clients, a dietitian's patients, a creator's followers. Before choosing a product, write down who will buy it and why they would choose you over a brand they already know.

## 2. Choose one product to start

Launch with one or two products, not ten. A single strong product with good reviews is easier to market, cheaper to stock and quicker to reorder. Protein, multivitamins and wellness gummies are common starting points, but the right choice depends on your audience.

## 3. Pick a verified manufacturer

Check licences, visit the factory, test samples and understand the minimum order quantity before you commit. We wrote a full checklist on [how to choose a supplement manufacturer](/blog/how-to-choose-a-supplement-manufacturer).

## 4. Get your licences and trademark in order

As the brand owner, you will usually need your own FSSAI licence alongside your manufacturer's. Marketplaces such as Amazon and Flipkart also ask for licence details before listing supplements. Apply for a trademark on your brand name early, so you aren't forced to rebrand after you've built a following.

## 5. Make the label compliant

Supplement labels in India must follow FSSAI's labelling rules and its regulations for health supplements and nutraceuticals. These cover mandatory declarations, ingredient and nutrition information, and what health claims you can and can't make. Have every label checked before printing. A reprint costs far more than a review.

## 6. Build the brand and the store

This is your brand name, story, logo and packaging, plus a website that takes orders and marketplace listings that show up in search. Good product photos and a clear explanation of who the product is for matter more than a long list of features.

## 7. Plan the launch, then keep going

Line up content, early reviews and a starting ad budget before launch day. After launch, track which channels bring buyers, reorder before you run out, and talk to your first customers. Their feedback shapes your second product.
